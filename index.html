Calculation basis

All results refer to block fuel onboard at A before taxi. OFP Fuel means the minimum block fuel from the operational flight plan. Maximum total departure fuel is the total onboard before taxi, not the quantity added by the fuel truck.

First sector cap: lowest of tank capacity, certified ramp limit − ZFW, effective A take-off limit − ZFW + taxi A, and effective B landing limit − ZFW + taxi A + A–B airborne burn. Effective limits are the lower of the aircraft certified limit and entered OPT limit.

Diversion adds a separate alternative branch: effective diversion landing limit − first-sector ZFW + taxi A + A–B burn + diversion burn. It also adds the minimum fuel needed to arrive at the diversion airport with its entered reserve.

Onward sector adds: effective B take-off limit − onward ZFW + taxi A + A–B burn + B ground burn; and effective C landing limit − onward ZFW + all burn through C. Diversion and onward branches are alternatives and do not add their fuel requirements together.

Minimum block fuel is the greatest applicable entered fuel requirement translated back to departure A. Blank fields never mean zero. Results check arithmetic and entered limits; fuel policy, NOTAM, runway performance, weather and MEL assessments remain external.
