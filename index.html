Calculation basis & scope

Maximum block fuel at A is the lowest of: A take-off limit − outbound ZFW + A taxi; B landing limit − outbound ZFW + A taxi + outbound airborne burn; B take-off limit − return ZFW + A taxi + outbound burn + B ground burn; A return landing limit − return ZFW + A taxi + outbound burn + B ground burn + return airborne burn; and usable tank capacity.

Minimum block fuel at A is the greater of: outbound minimum block fuel; and A taxi + outbound burn + required landing fuel at B; and A taxi + outbound burn + B ground burn + minimum return take-off fuel.

A is also the alternate for B. Return and diversion can have different burns and payloads: diversion-specific weight and fuel checks are not calculated here. This prototype checks entered limits only; confirm formulas and inputs against your approved planning method before operational use.

Taxi is separate from airborne burn. B ground burn includes all fuel consumed between landing at B and take-off from B. No uplift at B, fuel-density conversion, performance lookup or economic tankering penalty is modelled.
